# Taste Profile — AdvPulse

> Project-specific aesthetic decisions. Cross-project opinions live in the root `taste-profile.md`.
> Seeded 2026-04-08, split out of the cross-project profile 2026-08-28.

---

## Emotional target

Data-forward, structurally precise, professional without being corporate. It should feel obviously faster and more modern than the legacy incumbents at first glance, and it should feel premium despite costing a tenth of them.

---

## Project decisions that override or specialize the cross-project profile

- **Dark theme, layered surfaces.** Solid dark grounds.
- **No glass, frosted, or neumorphic treatment.** The cross-project profile carries glass as a soft pattern; this product explicitly rejects it. Traditional and data-forward.
- **Large border radius on containers.** Soft, rounded — a project decision, not a universal one.
- **Ghost/outline buttons as the default.** Filled reserved for a single primary action, plus a greyscale filled style for dense controls.
- **Two greens with two semantic jobs** — brand green for accent, active states, and machine-executed actions; success green for completion. Blue reserved for in-progress badges only.
- **Monospace logs** for machine transparency. "Watching the machine work" is the intended feeling.
- **Two layout families** — spacious full-screen chat, and dense split-panel. Mode switching is intentional, not a fallback.
- **Progress is a checkpoint log, never a spinner or a progress bar.** The backend knows what it just did and does not know how far along it is. A bar would invent the second thing; a spinner would discard the first.

## Copy rules

Dry and factual, Stripe register. No em dashes, no exclamation points, no hype adjectives, no tricolons. Sourceable claims only. Spelling is "advisor".

## What worked

Strong type hierarchy carrying the organizational load. Selective accent at roughly 10% of surface. Monospace run logs as the transparency device. Splitting the product system from the brand system rather than letting them blur.

## What didn't

Recorded at `/retro`.

## Carried forward to cross-project memory

Accent restraint · type-as-hierarchy · panel separation for density management · progress as an honest account rather than a fabricated percentage.
